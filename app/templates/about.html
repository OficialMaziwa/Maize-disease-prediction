The Problem

Maize diseases cause up to 50% crop loss in Sub-Saharan Africa. Late detection and limited access to agricultural extension services leave many farmers without timely solutions.

Diseases We Detect

🍂 Common Rust
Reddish-brown pustules on leaves causing reduced yield
🌿 Gray Leaf Spot
Rectangular gray lesions causing premature leaf death
🌱 Northern Leaf Blight
Long cigar-shaped lesions that reduce photosynthesis
